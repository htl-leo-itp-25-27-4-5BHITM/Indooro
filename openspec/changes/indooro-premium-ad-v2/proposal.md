## Why

V1's 65-second film explains Indooro but gives the shopper little presence. The current V2 motion-graphics plan solves the live-action dependency and establishes a human-centered route story. Its 13-shot, 28-second version still risks reading as a polished feature demonstration: the opening is static, the phone-to-store handoff is a matched cut, and the product/brand payoff is conventional. This final creative revision strengthens those exact points without replacing the foundation.

## What Changes

- Refine the selected concept to a **26-second / 780-frame / 30 fps German, 16:9 premium motion graphics advertisement** with nine shots. The same sculptural shopper needs milk, activates Indooro, follows one route and finds a generic carton.
- Make three documented signature moments the film's backbone: **Chaos becomes direction** (architectural camera and selective clarity), **Enter the map** (continuous phone-screen-to-store camera), and **Destination becomes identity** (precise product signal and route-to-brand ending).
- Give the route one consistent UI/map/world/destination/brand motion and sound language. Improve the human from exposed primitives to a premium adult-proportioned clothed silhouette with limited, reproducible pose animation.
- Keep Remotion as master edit, crisp UI, route/compositing, audio and export system; prefer short scripted Blender store/figure/phone passes with a Remotion 2.5D fallback. No live-action footage, filmed actor, location, C4D or remote video-generation service is required.
- Add an explicit **four-prototype gate**: opening/clarity, phone/search, continuous phone-to-store, destination/brand at 1280×720 draft quality. Full navigation and complete-film production wait for separate prototype acceptance.
- Update the storyboard, VO/music/SFX, shot/technical/risk/review documents and specs. This originally ended at a planning gate; the user's later approval authorized only the four draft prototypes, now recorded in `indooro-ad-v2/`.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `premium-ad-creative-direction`: Shopper-centered 26-second ad and truthful claim boundaries.
- `premium-ad-visual-storytelling`: Nine-shot journey, sculptural user and product payoff.
- `premium-ad-motion-design`: Three signature moments, continuous phone crossing and one route motion language.
- `premium-ad-user-experience`: Readable illustrative search/map flow and visible shopper agency.
- `premium-ad-audio-production`: Minimal German VO, evolving route sound, electronic score and listening review.
- `premium-ad-technical-production`: Remotion master, scripted Blender passes, 2.5D fallback and four-prototype gate.
- `premium-ad-quality-assurance`: Human review, rights, reproducibility and explicit approval gates.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a proposed film representation; it changes no app, API or deployment behavior. V1 film specs remain historical.

## Impact

Planning, accepted prototypes and the **rejected** rough cut stay in this existing OpenSpec change; V1 and its tag/exports remain intact. The isolated `indooro-ad-v2/` project contains seven draft scene passages, a 780-frame 720p composition and a shared illustrative layout. The 2026-09-30 visual review found the market too empty and the shopper/material quality unsuitable for presentation; a separate look test now explores stocked retail geometry, scanned goods and a one-second walking camera sample. This is not a revised film. The phone search/map/route claim must still match current app/spec references; the store-space line is an editorial visualization, not a shipping AR or measured accuracy claim. Official mark and authorized voice/music remain future assets. No filming dependency exists. Picture lock and final rendering remain separate gates.
