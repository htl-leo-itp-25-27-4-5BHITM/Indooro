## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Demonstrable customer interaction
The generated phone sequence SHALL show one search term, one selected product and a route to its walkable shelf access point, using an approved and explicitly illustrative UI state based on current app/spec references.

#### Scenario: Search to map
- **WHEN** the user taps the selected product in the planned film
- **THEN** the screen changes to a map/route state associated with that same product and a coherent physical destination

### Requirement: Human agency
The same stylized adult-proportioned shopper SHALL remain identifiable through need, phone lift, turn, route-following movement and reach; the figure SHALL require no photoreal face, complex hand rig or filmed performer. Route graphics SHALL remain a visual metaphor and SHALL not imply a shipping AR feature unless verified.

#### Scenario: Physical navigation beat
- **WHEN** the route appears in a supermarket shot
- **THEN** the simplified shopper visibly turns, follows the route and reaches the selected product in the generated store
