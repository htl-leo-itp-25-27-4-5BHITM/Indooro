## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Demonstrable customer interaction
The phone sequence SHALL show one search term, one selected product and a route to its walkable shelf access point, using an approved actual or explicitly illustrative UI state.

#### Scenario: Search to map
- **WHEN** the user taps the selected product in the planned film
- **THEN** the screen changes to a map/route state associated with that same product and a coherent physical destination

### Requirement: Human agency
The person SHALL be visible or physically present through continuity of hand, gaze, movement and reach; route overlays SHALL remain a visual metaphor and SHALL not imply a shipping AR feature unless verified.

#### Scenario: Physical navigation beat
- **WHEN** the route appears in a supermarket shot
- **THEN** the customer visibly makes the route-guided movement and later reaches the selected product
