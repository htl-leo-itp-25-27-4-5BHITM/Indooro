## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complete physical-to-digital-to-physical story
The film SHALL show a recognizable physical problem, a motivated phone interaction, a clear route response, purposeful human movement and a physical product discovery in that order.

#### Scenario: Shot-order inspection
- **WHEN** a reviewer examines the timed rough cut
- **THEN** the app's action visibly changes the same customer's path and leads to the wanted product

### Requirement: Readable interaction and paced contrast
The film SHALL give the product name, search result and route enough visible time to be read at half-resolution preview, while varying fast movement with at least one controlled clarity beat.

#### Scenario: Half-resolution preview review
- **WHEN** the preview is played at 1280×720 on a typical laptop
- **THEN** the search term, selected result, route destination and final brand message remain identifiable without pausing
