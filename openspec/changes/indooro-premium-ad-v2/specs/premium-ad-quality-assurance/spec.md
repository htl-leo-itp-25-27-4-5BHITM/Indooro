## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Evidence-based visual, narrative and rights review
The production SHALL verify actual preview/final media, shot continuity, legibility, claim accuracy, footage/voice/music rights and the customer's story through human review.

#### Scenario: Rough-cut review
- **WHEN** a complete rough cut exists
- **THEN** a reviewer watches it muted and with sound, records time-coded notes and confirms each asset's release or license before final quality rendering

### Requirement: Explicit production gates
Planning SHALL end at `AWAITING USER APPROVAL — INDOORO V2 CONCEPT`; implementation, media generation, purchases and final rendering SHALL remain pending until explicit user approval, with later rough-cut and final-master checkpoints.

#### Scenario: Planning change completed
- **WHEN** OpenSpec artifacts and review package are complete
- **THEN** no V2 animation project or prototype exists and all implementation/render tasks remain unchecked
