# Final creative audit — before prototype production

**Scope:** planning-only review of the 28-second motion-graphics V2 concept against the newly requested premium ad standard. No new scene, render, sound or prototype was made. The overall Remotion/Blender, stylized store/shopper, phone, route and German VO foundation remains.

| Remaining weakness in prior plan | Change in this revision | Why the ad improves |
|---|---|---|
| Paused-shopper opening stated the problem but did not make scale/choice visceral | Low architectural aisle passage, one turn, crane to small shopper; phone activation selects light/detail without moving shelves | Viewer feels complexity before Indooro resolves it; change has a visual cause |
| Phone-to-store route was a matched graphic cut | Continuous scripted camera crosses the phone screen; map footprints extrude into shelves; SVG/floor route share geometry and overlap | Creates one memorable product-specific hero moment rather than a familiar transition |
| Product receive/outline and separate end card were conventional | Final floor-level route rounds corner, precise access marker and thin shelf plane identify milk; shopper reach resolves marker; residual line creates/frames approved brand | Product and identity become one visual payoff that closes the route's journey |
| Primitive faceless figure risked looking like a tutorial mannequin | Adult sculptural clothing volumes, matte graphite/fabric/ceramic treatment, limited poses, no face/rig complexity; partial framing fallback | Human remains present without toy or uncanny detail, within scripted Blender capability |
| Navigation risked repetitive map views/walk loop | Low chase, lateral human reveal, brief elevated turn and matched screen direction | Eight seconds of purposeful viewpoint variation, continuous route anchor |
| Route identity was mostly color/glow | Defined core width, rounded corners, draw/easing, marker ripple, destination pulse, spatial ribbon and residual brand path | One recognizable Indooro object across phone, world, product and mark |
| 13 shots / 28 seconds left weak connective beats | Nine shots / 26 seconds / 780 frames, static final identity for 75 frames | Energy rises while search, destination and claim retain readability |
| VO described visible events and sound followed generic arc | Minimal Script A (~5.2 s spoken) and 13-bar score; one digital-to-spatial-to-brand route sound | Picture carries the hero moments; sound makes them recognizably Indooro |

## Unresolved creative and technical risks

The opening could feel like a generic architectural fly-through or obscure the person. A premium sculptural shopper may still look synthetic. The phone crossing requires accurate screen projection, camera matrices, map/store geometry and alpha/depth handling; the 2.5D continuous-camera fallback must be judged by the same idea, not treated as a shortcut. The destination/brand line cannot distort an official mark. At 720p, search/result, target carton and 2.5-second brand hold must survive a single normal-speed view. Sound's two-note motif and a synthetic German voice cannot be judged from documentation. Blender is not currently in `PATH`; it remains untested. Claims about UI and spatial navigation need comparison with current app/spec references, and brand/voice/music rights remain open.

## Recommendation and next gate

The **26-second, nine-shot version** is ready for a decision on **prototype production only**. Authorize exactly P1 opening/clarity, P2 phone/search, P3 continuous phone-to-store and P4 destination/brand at 1280×720 draft quality, with temporary cleared sound/scratch VO where useful. Review normal-speed video muted and with sound, inspect geometry continuity and human/product legibility, then explicitly accept or revise those prototypes. **Do not build navigation shots 05–07 or the full film until this gate passes.** No implementation permission is inferred from approval of the broad creative direction.
