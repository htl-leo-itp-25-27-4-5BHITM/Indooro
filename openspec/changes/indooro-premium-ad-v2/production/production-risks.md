# Production risks and decision triggers

| Risk | Trigger | Response |
|---|---|---|
| No authorized human/store footage | Release/location unavailable before shoot | Redesign a controlled set and review concept again; avoid incoherent stock montage |
| UI does not match current app | Captured flow differs from proposed search/map | Update storyboard or label illustrative UI; never imply a shipping screen falsely |
| Mixed-reality transition looks artificial | Tracking, hand occlusion or lighting fails in 720p prototype | Simplify to a precise graphic match cut; do not spend full film budget before proof |
| 32 s is too fast | First-time viewers miss product or route at 720p | Add 1–3 s to result/route/discovery holds; retain 25–40 s boundary |
| Natural German VO unavailable | Scratch read sounds synthetic or unclear | Cast alternate performer; licensed TTS only after human audition and rights review |
| Music masks the story | VO intelligibility or product foley fails on phone | Reduce/duck music, simplify arrangement, recheck on devices |
| Asset rights unclear | Missing release, editorial-only stock or distribution mismatch | Exclude/relicense asset before final; do not publish ambiguous media |
| Brand identity not approved | V1 glyph differs from official mark | Hold final brand animation; secure approved master art |
| Generated footage inconsistency | Hands/packaging or location continuity changes | Prefer controlled filming; use AI only for non-critical transitional plates if approved |

Risk owner is the future producer/creative reviewer; all items are currently open until production decisions are made.
