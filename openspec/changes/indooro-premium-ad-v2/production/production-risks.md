# Production risks and decision triggers — revised creative

| Risk | Evidence to gather in approved prototypes | Response / fallback |
|---|---|---|
| Opening feels like a generic store fly-through or viewers lose orientation | P1 normal-speed first-viewer test misses shopper or meaning of activation | Shorten low passage, widen human reveal, simplify shelf rhythm; preserve visible hierarchy shift with 2.5D layers if 3D camera fails |
| Shopper resembles tutorial mannequin, stock human or cartoon | P1/P4 silhouette and reach read poorly at 720p | Continuous clothing volumes, matte materials, fewer poses, better lighting; crop to shoulder/upper body or partial reach while retaining person at key beats |
| Phone-to-store is a disguised cut or visibly drifts | P3 frame-by-frame check of screen projection and 12–18-frame route overlap | Fix camera/quadrilateral/layout transform; use uninterrupted Remotion 2.5D virtual camera if Blender composite remains unstable |
| Map polygons and shelves fail to correspond | P3 route seems to teleport or intersect shelf | Generate both from one layout, validate coordinate transform and walkable clearance before visual polish |
| Render time exceeds practical budget | P1/P3 short draft sequence is too slow or unstable | Reduce shelf detail/samples/pass count, cache sequences, then choose 2.5D fallback; C4D is not the rescue plan |
| Destination feels like a generic green outline or ambiguous target | P4 first viewers cannot identify exact milk carton/reach | Use route circle at access point, narrow vertical light, selective contrast, clearer carton and held reaction; remove excess glow |
| Route-to-brand invents or distorts official logo | P4 brand prototype conflicts with approved mark | Let route frame the approved wordmark instead of morphing its geometry |
| Nine shots / 26 s become too fast | `Milch`, shopper turn, found product or final claim missed in 720p viewing | Retime clarity beats within 25–27 s and update all frame/cue tables; avoid adding features or arbitrary cuts |
| Spatial route implies shipping AR or unsupported accuracy | Claims review reads store line literally as app feature | Keep UI and editorial world distinct, use illustrative disclosure, remove metrics and check current app truth |
| Sonic route motif sounds like stock whooshes or VO masks it | P3/P4 human listening on phone/laptop/headphones fails | One evolving route timbre, fewer cues, alternate authorized German read, stronger silence/ducking; do not approve from waveform metrics alone |
| Brand/voice/music rights remain unverified | Approved asset or license is missing | Hold production use/final delivery until source and rights are documented |

Blender 5.2.2 CLI rendered all three short 3D passes; Remotion rendered all four 720p drafts. Sampled frames show a still somewhat rigid shopper and a P3 transition that relies on a vector-map/3D overlap rather than finished per-pixel geometry extrusion. The P4 marker and provisional brand treatment are visible, but normal-speed first-viewer and listening tests remain outstanding. The complete film, final voice/music and approved logo remain unproduced. See [prototype review](../../../../indooro-ad-v2/previews/prototype-review.md).
