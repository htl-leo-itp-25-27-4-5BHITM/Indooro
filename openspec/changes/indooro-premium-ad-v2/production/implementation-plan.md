# Future implementation plan — begins only after explicit approval

**Indicative effort:** 10–16 focused working days across preproduction (1–2), filming/ingest (1–2), UI/route/compositing prototypes (2–3), full edit/motion (3–4), voice/music/mix (1–2), review/revisions/QA (2–3). This is not a quote, schedule commitment or evidence that assets exist. One small team can parallelize sound preparation with approved footage editing, but concept approval, rights and rough-cut reviews are gates.

1. Confirm open decisions: actor/location, UI source, identity, Script A or alternative, channels/aspect ratios and spending cap. Record rights before media use.
2. Set up V2 package and deterministic 30 fps timeline. Write route/shot continuity checks where they catch a real risk. Keep V1 untouched.
3. Prototype shots 01–02, 04–08, 09 and 15–16 as short 720p ranges. Prove legible search and phone-to-aisle match cut before building all travel footage.
4. Build an audio-first 32 s rough cut with real scratch VO, temp cleared/original cue and location sound labeled temporary. First-viewer test the muted story and spoken comprehension.
5. Refine footage/graphics, approve picture lock, then produce/clear final VO/music/SFX and mix stems. Listen with human reviewers on multiple devices.
6. Render 1080p master only after refined-cut approval; probe technical properties, inspect sampled encoded frames and boundaries, verify claims/rights and collect final acceptance. Archive OpenSpec only after actual verified delivery.
