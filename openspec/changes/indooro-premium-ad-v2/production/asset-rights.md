# Rights, services and cost research (checked 2026-09-29)

**Preferred route:** Film one consenting person in an authorized unbranded store or dressed set; record original foley and commission/produce original short music. Obtain written actor likeness/voice release, location permission, brand approval and package/artwork clearance before shooting or release. Student project status does not itself grant advertising rights. Keep signed documents outside public Git and record a non-sensitive rights status in the asset manifest.

| Option | Suitability and rights gate | Known external cost / uncertainty |
|---|---|---|
| Internal/local shoot | Best continuity and authentic human response. Requires actor, store/location and visible product permissions; no external media license if all rights are granted. | Equipment/location/performer cost depends on availability; no reliable price without a quote. |
| Adobe Stock footage/music | Fallback for a missing insert, but different actors and store geography can break the 16-shot story. Check commercial ad, audience, redistribution, editorial-only and audio channel restrictions for each asset. [Adobe Stock license terms](https://stock.adobe.com/license-terms) describe video/visual and music scopes; audio extended rights can be required for TV/radio, paid streaming, apps or physical POS. | Per-asset or subscription price depends on chosen clip/license; obtain checkout quote before approval. |
| ElevenLabs German TTS | Fallback if a human read is unavailable and the voice sounds natural in context. Do not clone a real person without explicit rights/consent. [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing) listed Starter at **US$6/month** and a commercial license when checked; [their help article](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform) says free outputs lack commercial rights. Terms and account status must be rechecked at purchase/use. | US$6/month is a current reference for Starter, excluding taxes; generation/use and voice rights still need confirmation. No account or paid plan is assumed. |
| AI-generated footage, including services such as Higgsfield | Last resort for non-human transition plates only. Face/hand consistency, product/package stability, camera match, licensing and disclosure need shot-by-shot verification; no service capability or account is assumed. | No reliable project cost without approved provider, plan and generation count. |
| Original programmatic map/phone graphics | Strong for controllable route and screen imagery; source truth and brand approval still required. | No asset purchase; developer/compositor time is the main cost. |

**No purchase or upload is authorized by this plan.** The selected concept does not depend on ElevenLabs, Higgsfield, stock, or a paid sound library. If the distribution channel later includes TV, POS or paid streaming, recheck all footage/music licenses and release language. Record license URLs, invoice IDs and use restrictions before final rendering; do not commit private contracts or credentials.
