# Rights and service choices — revised 2026-09-30

The selected spot uses original programmatic geometry, vector UI and a generic milk carton. It needs **no actor likeness release, filming location agreement, stock supermarket clip, photoreal human model or commercial footage generation service**. Confirm Indooro logo ownership/approved use, retain Inter's OFL notice and document any voice, music or sample license. Do not buy, upload or generate production media during planning.

| Option | Role and rights gate | Cost/availability note |
|---|---|---|
| Remotion/React/SVG/CSS | Master composition and complete 2.5D fallback; local source must be versioned | V1 has Remotion installed; future V2 package/version still needs approved setup |
| Blender Python | Preferred short scripted 3D passes; no external model library required | [Blender license](https://www.blender.org/about/license/) permits use and ownership of created artwork. Blender executable is not found in current `PATH`; preflight/install only after approval. |
| Cinema 4D | No role in the selected workflow | App is visible locally, but licensing/automation is unnecessary and unverified |
| German TTS | Practical AI-producible VO option if natural and commercial rights are confirmed; do not clone a real person | [ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing) listed Starter at US$6/month when checked previously; [their rights guidance](https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform) says free output lacks commercial rights. Recheck at use; no account assumed. |
| Original music/SFX | Preferred: code-based synthesis or commissioned original cue with clear ownership | No fixed service price; production time and listening review remain |
| Licensed samples/music | Optional only if original audio is insufficient | Check exact ad/channel license and invoice before use; no price assumed |
| Stock/AI video | Optional future alternate campaign, **not** an asset for this concept | Would add continuity and rights risk; no service capability/cost assumed |

No external service is essential to the *picture*. A natural, rights-cleared German voice and human listening remain quality requirements. If the final distribution includes TV, retail POS or paid streaming, recheck the selected audio license for that use. Keep contracts/credentials outside public Git and only a non-sensitive approval record in the asset manifest.
