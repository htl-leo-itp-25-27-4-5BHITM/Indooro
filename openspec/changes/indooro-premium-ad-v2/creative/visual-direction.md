# Visual direction — premium digital world

**Palette and materials:** Retain V1 graphite `#071117`, panel `#11242B`, off-white `#F4F7F5` and mint `#63E6B4`; use amber `#E8B86D` only for the first uncertain search trace. Store shelves are beveled charcoal monoliths with thin cool edge light, not photographic grocery inventory. Floor is a deep matte plane with controlled reflections under the route, no neon grid everywhere. The shopper is a darker graphite ceramic/soft-metal silhouette with a subtle brighter outline against shelves. Milk is a simple warm-white carton form and label, unmistakable without branded packaging. Skin/cloth realism is deliberately absent.

**Figure language:** Adult proportions (roughly 7–7.5 heads), rounded but not toy-like. Head has no face, torso/limbs are simple connected forms; no smiling mask or mascot details. The body begins slightly folded/paused, lifts a small phone slab, turns toward the mint path, moves with a restrained two-pose gait or camera-aided translation, and reaches the target. The upper-body gesture is readable in silhouette at 720p. Avoid full-body close-ups that expose primitive joints; no detailed hands. A 2.5D SVG silhouette with matching poses is the Remotion fallback.

**Phone and UI:** Phone is a premium hero object, centered only while the viewer needs to read it. A 3D beveled body/reflection pass can be Blender-generated; screen content remains crisp SVG/React in Remotion so text is editable and not baked into a low-resolution 3D texture. Search `Milch`, one result and one route are the only demo interactions. A small `Konzeptdarstellung` treatment or delivery disclosure accompanies illustrative UI; verify exact copy with current iOS specs. No long static phone hold.

**Camera and composition:** Use three reusable virtual camera setups: oblique store wide (~35 mm equivalent), near-overhead route view (~45 mm), and phone hero (~60 mm). Camera movement is restrained but purposeful: push toward paused figure, snap/arc to phone, travel along path, then settle at product and brand. Shelves create parallax and negative space ahead of the shopper after activation. Keep screen direction consistent: shopper and route progress left-to-right until the product; do not spin the world arbitrarily. Blur belongs to moving camera/objects, never the clarity hold of the UI.

**Typography and graphics:** Inter is proposed for UI/claim. Use no chapter numbers, A* labels or long paragraphs. One main route glyph, one search word, one destination and final `Finde deinen Weg.` Brand card holds fully legible for 2.5 s. Route is a 6–10 px rounded stroke at 1080p with controlled bloom and a grounded 3D counterpart; markers are few and intentional. UI remains readable in a 720p review render.

**Three key frames to pursue after approval:** (1) Small paused figure in a large sculptural aisle; the absence of a path makes the need readable. (2) Oversized floating phone at an oblique angle; `Milch` result and touch cue remain crisp as a mint line leaves the screen. (3) The same figure follows the line through shelves and stops at a bright generic carton; then phone and route resolve into the Indooro mark. This is a concept description, not an existing render.
