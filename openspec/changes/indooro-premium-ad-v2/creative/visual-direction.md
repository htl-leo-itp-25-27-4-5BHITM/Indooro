# Visual direction

**Physical world:** Warm-neutral supermarket practicals (target 4000–4500 K) and natural skin exposure, with dark graphite clothing that silhouettes the hand against a bright shelf. Shoot on a long aisle with converging shelf lines; keep packaging generic and art-direct a milk carton at one reachable shelf. No recognizable retailer sign. Shallow depth for hesitation, deeper focus when the route clarifies direction. One actor walks screen-left to screen-right after the app opens; reverse travel is disallowed unless a motivated turn is shown.

**Digital world:** Retain V1's graphite `#071117` and mint `#63E6B4` as anchors. Warm shelf highlights shift toward neutral white as the route resolves. Amber `#E8B86D` appears only as a short uncertainty trace, not a competing brand color. Keep skin tones natural; do not wash live footage teal. UI backgrounds use dark panels and high contrast off-white text. The route is a 6–10 px rounded mint stroke at 1080p, with a quiet glow and minimal particles; the product marker is a solid ring/label, not a hologram.

**Type and identity:** Inter remains the proposed UI/VO-support type; use at most two headline states and a final approved Indooro lockup. No numbered chapter labels or algorithm chips. Screen text must be readable at a 720p review export; keep one search term (`Milch`) and one selected result visible. Final card: wordmark/glyph and `Finde deinen Weg.` on graphite, with 2 s fully legible hold. Verify the actual approved logo against `logos/`; V1's vector glyph is a reference, not automatically the official mark.

**Camera:** 35 mm equivalent waist-level aisle medium shot for human context; 50–70 mm equivalent close hand/phone insert; overhead/near-macro for the selection; 28–35 mm tracking side/three-quarter shot for purposeful travel; one stable shelf reach. Avoid orbiting the phone for its own sake. Motion blur is camera/subject motivated, shutter near 180° for filmed footage, with digital blur matched to the photographed shutter. Stabilization must preserve handheld urgency without floating.

**Composition:** Put the uncertain customer in a crowded third, with shelves interrupting sight lines. At route ignition, clear a directional corridor of negative space ahead of their gaze. At the product reach, align hand, carton and destination marker; then remove overlay. Key shots have one visual priority: actor, screen, route, product or brand.

**Reference direction:** premium product clarity, rhythmic editorial energy and tangible location sound, without reproducing any particular technology/automotive/sports brand's execution or assets. Key visual 1: fingertip taps a large readable `Milch` result while aisle bokeh reflects in glass. Key visual 2: mint line exits the bottom-right phone map at the cut and matches a shelf-edge line moving in the same direction. Key visual 3: route resolves at a real carton; hand enters, grabs, overlay disappears, and the action completes the story.
