# Transition choreography — 26-second film

1. **Complexity → direction, frames 0–119.** A low camera passes aisle faces, turns, rises to the paused shopper, then the phone lift at frame 75 subtracts peripheral light and sound. Shelves do not move. A restrained position ripple and mint core enter; no arbitrary wipe. See signature shot 1.
2. **Shopper → phone/search, frames 120–179.** Camera maintains the phone's motion from the lift, reaches a near-frontal readable view, shows `Milch`, one result and one selection. Result becomes map without a montage of menu screens. Shopper shoulder keeps scale and agency.
3. **Phone → world, frames 180–299.** One scripted camera advances toward the screen, aligns to route tangent, crosses the screen plane and descends into the store. A shared route and map footprints become the floor line and shelf volumes. Blender supplies camera/geometry/mattes; Remotion supplies crisp projected UI and overlapping route. Check the 12–18 overlap frames for pixel-level continuity. No blur-hidden cut. See signature shot 2.
4. **World navigation, frames 300–539.** Low chase, lateral human reveal, then a brief elevated turn/descend. Path direction, camera movement and shopper travel match across cuts. Shelves occlude route correctly. Each shot changes viewpoint for a story reason.
5. **Product → brand, frames 540–779.** Route rounds the final corner, slows into a precise marker and thin shelf light; shopper finds milk. Only after contact does a visible route segment continue forward into darkness and create or frame the approved brand element. Wordmark and `Finde deinen Weg.` are static from frame 705 for 2.5 s. See signature shot 3.

Use one easing family: brief acceleration, near-linear path travel, controlled deceleration, no bounce. Motion blur is limited to moving camera/geometry; search/result/map holds and brand remain crisp. The route is one object from UI to end frame, not five similar effects. A transition fails review if it hides the chosen item, breaks camera/path continuity, makes shelves appear to rearrange, or implies an unverified in-app AR feature.
