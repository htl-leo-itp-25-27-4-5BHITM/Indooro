# Selected concept — Der Weg wird sichtbar

**One sentence:** A customer in an unfamiliar supermarket wants milk; Indooro turns the question into a visible path and the path into a real product in hand.

**Audience:** First-time customers and observers who need to grasp Indooro in one watch. The film sells confidence and ease, not an algorithm. **Core promise:** `Finde deinen Weg.` Product proof is the same milk item in the search, route destination and shelf. No retailer mark, quantified savings or positioning accuracy appears.

**Narrative:** 0–3.2 s physical hesitation; 3.2–7 s phone action; 7–12 s search and result; 12–17 s route extends from map into aisle; 17–24 s the customer follows the indicated direction; 24–28 s milk is found; 28–32 s brand resolution. The hand, clothing, aisle direction and milk package stay consistent. The route overlay is explicitly cinematic visualization, not an in-app AR screen. The phone UI must be verified against the app or visibly treated as a concept composite.

**Emotional movement:** small uncertainty → precise decision → forward momentum → quiet confidence. Energy comes from an earned change in pace: a fast first glance, a still readable search result, a clean route ignition, travel cuts on musical subdivisions and a calm product/brand landing.

**Approval dependencies:** actor and location permissions, product packaging without unwanted brand prominence, approved Indooro identity, UI source choice, narration performance, music/SFX rights and distribution. Without a filmable aisle, revise the concept before production rather than manufacturing inconsistent footage.
