# Selected concept — Der Weg wird sichtbar (motion graphics edition)

**One sentence:** In a sculptural digital supermarket, a shopper needs milk; Indooro transforms an unreadable field of shelves into one clear route, which the same shopper follows to the product.

The shopper is a **faceless, adult-proportioned graphite figure**, built from a small set of beveled primitives with soft mint rim light. A paused posture, phone lift, decisive turn, forward travel and restrained reach tell the emotional story without facial acting, fingers, lip sync, cloth simulation or cartoon bounce. Show the figure in 3/4, rear and overhead views; repeat its silhouette at key moments so the user does not disappear when the interface takes over. In a Remotion-only fallback, equivalent layered SVG/2.5D silhouette poses retain the same blocking.

The environment is a modular, unbranded aisle geometry generated from one illustrative walkable layout. Shelves begin as dense dark forms, then recede/part when the route lights up. This is a visual change in clarity, not a claim that physical shelves move. The phone is the hero object: a compact 3D body or layered vector shell, with a large crisp Remotion-rendered screen. The touch is a precise light/ripple cue associated with the figure's phone lift; no photoreal hand is needed. Search `Milch`, choose a result, see the map and follow the same endpoint to a stylized milk carton. The product is an original generic form/icon; no retailer or packaging mark.

**28-second arc:** uncertainty 0–3 s; activation/search 3–8 s; route 8–13 s; guided movement 13–21 s; found 21–24 s; brand 24–28 s. The route is a mint stroke through shared store geometry and the visual signature of the ad. It is an editorial visualization, not a claim of shipping AR guidance or measured location accuracy. No filmed footage is required. Remotion is central; Blender enhances select 3D shots if available, with Remotion-only equivalents documented.

**Approval dependencies:** revised concept, official Indooro mark, exact illustrative UI/copy, voice source/rights, intended channels/aspect ratios and optional budget. Actor, location and real-footage access are not dependencies. This remains a plan; no V2 scene, asset render or audio has been produced.
