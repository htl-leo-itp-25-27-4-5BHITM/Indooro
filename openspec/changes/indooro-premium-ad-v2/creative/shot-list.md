# Practical shot list for future local shoot

No location, actor or crew has been booked. Shoot extra handles (3 s before/after action), clean plate and room tone for each setup. Obtain written actor and location permission before recording; avoid visible retailer marks and bystanders. Aim for 4K 30 fps acquisition or higher resolution for stabilization/crops while editing to 1080p/30 fps.

| Setup / storyboard shots | Framing / movement | Required action and continuity | Capture needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| A / 01, 09, 12 | 35 mm medium, tripod-to-short track, same aisle direction | Entrance hesitation, then rightward choice, later same shelf corner | Wide clean plate, shelf/ground tracking points, generic signage |
| B / 02 | 50 mm profile close-up, slight handheld | Search gaze, hand-to-pocket action | Matching eyeline and lighting; no accidental milk in background |
| C / 03–07, 11 | 50–70 mm phone/hand, controlled over-shoulder | Open app, type `Milch`, result, tap, map glance; same hand and phone orientation | Practical blank/reference screen, glare control, thumb-clear take, screen tracking/calibration, separate clean inserts |
| D / 08 | Locked phone macro and aligned aisle plate | Phone route exits down/right; shelf edge continues movement | Camera match, lens/height notes, color chart, clean plate for composite |
| E / 10 | 28–35 mm lateral track | Customer walks right with basket, consistent speed/wardrobe | Foreground wipe object, clean plate, footsteps/cloth wild tracks |
| F / 13–14 | 50 mm shelf reach then 35 mm medium | Reach exact milk carton at walkable side of shelf, place in basket | Matching package across screen/shelf, multiple natural takes, carton/basket sound |

Minimum media checklist: actor release; location permission; permissions for any recognizable package, art, music or visible person; static shots of phone, shelf, route surfaces and brand-safe environment. For a smaller production, A/B/E/F can be shot in one dressed aisle, C/D on the same day with a controllable phone rig. If a real supermarket refuses filming, use an authorized mock shelf set and rewrite the location shots consistently; do not splice unrelated stock locations.
