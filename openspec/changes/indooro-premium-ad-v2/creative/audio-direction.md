# Audio direction and cue strategy

**Premise:** The audience hears a place first, then an intelligible person and a route gaining momentum. V1's continuous no-VO synthesized bed is not the V2 music cue. A new four-stem workflow separates location sound, VO, music and designed UI/transition effects. Original composition is preferred; a licensed track may be considered only if it can follow the cue map and the specific advertisement rights are documented.

## 120 BPM / 16-bar proposed arc

| Bars / time | Musical behavior | Sound and narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 / 0–4 s | Filtered low pulse, sparse notes, room air; tension without melodrama | Real footsteps, clothing, first VO question. Keep room for “Aber wo?” |
| 3–6 / 4–12 s | Tight electronic percussion enters in steps, short restrained bass pulse, warm harmonic lift on result | One app-open/tap, search focus, result tone; duck around “Indooro zeigt dir das Regal.” |
| 7–8 / 12–16 s | Downbeat impact at route expansion, thin ascending synth line; avoid a long generic whoosh | Route activation leads image by a few frames; “Und den Weg dorthin” sits above beat. |
| 9–12 / 16–24 s | Fullest groove with controlled low end and syncopated high percussion; momentum from footsteps | Trolley, steps and one justified phone cue integrated rhythmically. No wall-to-wall interface pings. |
| 13–14 / 24–28 s | Kick drops out on hand-to-carton contact; warm chord release and brief breath of air | Shelf/carton/basket sounds foregrounded; “Ein Blick. Ein Griff.” |
| 15–16 / 28–32 s | Two-note original mnemonic, then decaying harmonic tail and resolved silence | `Indooro. Finde deinen Weg.` remains front and clear; no hard audio cutoff. |

## Production and acceptance

Create a cue sheet tied to exact frame timecode; edit picture to real scratch VO breath lengths. Use subtle foley recorded at the same location or original designed sounds. Spatial perspective changes with camera distance: close handling sounds in inserts, softer wider aisle ambience in medium shots. Avoid stock whoosh collections and repetitive pings. Run an explicit listening review on headphones, laptop and phone. Technical target for a web/social master: discuss delivery platform first, provisionally around −16 LUFS integrated stereo with true peak ≤−1 dBTP, no clipped sample, no unintended >0.4 s digital silence, dialogue clearly intelligible through small speakers and clean final tail. The number is a target for future mix, not a measurement of an existing V2 asset. Preserve 48 kHz stems, export AAC at suitable bitrate with the video and keep a lossless mix master. A signal pass cannot certify musical quality.

V1 audio audit is in `research/audio-audit.md`. Neither its WAV nor any new generated or purchased audio is included in this planning change.
