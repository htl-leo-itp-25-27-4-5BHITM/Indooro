The route in store space is editorial visualization, not in-app AR. 120 BPM supplies fourteen 2-second bars.

## Narrative and feasibility checks

The same figure appears in shots 01–03, 07–11 and optionally 12. Its need, app activation, route choice and product reach are visible without detailed acting. The same `Milch` target persists through screen, map and carton. Blender contributes depth in a few short passes; if unavailable, every B shot has an R equivalent with identical timing and staging. The phone-to-store transition is a matched graphic cut between known coordinate views, not a fragile photographed composite. At 720p, the result, completed route, figure turn, found carton and end card must remain identifiable. If the actual VO or viewer test needs more time, adjust holds within 25–30 s rather than adding new features.
