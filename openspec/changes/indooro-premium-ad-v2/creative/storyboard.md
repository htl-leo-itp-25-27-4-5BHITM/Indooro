## Rhythm and continuity checks

Shots 06–07 and 15–16 are the clarity anchors. Every product cue says milk; the route starts at a walkable position and ends beside the same carton. The actor's travel direction and wardrobe remain consistent. Bars 1–2 establish tension, 3–6 interaction, 7–12 navigation momentum, 13–14 discovery release, 15–16 brand signature. If the recorded VO or first-viewer preview needs more dwell, extend to 33–35 seconds by adding time to 06/07/13/16 rather than new feature scenes.
