# Concept exploration — revised for motion graphics

The earlier selected concept required a photographed actor, real supermarket, practical phone plate and filmed reach. Its core **need → search → route → discovery** survives; its production method does not. Three fully digital directions were compared:

| Direction | Strongest image | Human clarity / premium potential | Practical risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — Graphic silhouette | A flat 2.5D adult silhouette moves through an elegant layered shelf field while a mint route activates. | Highly controllable, fastest Remotion-only delivery; depth may resemble an explainer if composition stays diagrammatic. | Moderate: requires artful camera parallax and clear figure poses. | **Fallback** for every B shot. |
| B — “Der Weg wird sichtbar” | A sculptural clothed shopper is revealed by an architectural store camera; the viewer enters the phone map and follows one route to milk and brand. | One human story, three distinctive signature moments, premium depth with limited assets; best balance. | Moderate/high in phone projection and figure quality; four draft prototypes gate full production. | **Selected and refined.** |
| C — Full 3D spatial spectacle | Camera races through a large simulated supermarket with complex shelves, reflections and character animation in every shot. | High visual ceiling but the phone/user can be lost. | High: many models, long renders, continuity and rigging burden. | Reject as a full-film approach; borrow only one controlled architectural opening. |

**Production approaches:** Remotion alone can build A and all fallback shots with SVG/CSS/2.5D. Remotion + scripted Blender is the preferred execution of B: few 3D hero/environment passes, with Remotion owning story, UI, transitions and audio. A three-tool Remotion + Blender + Cinema 4D or generative-video chain offers no necessary benefit and adds fragile handoffs. Cinema 4D being installed does not make it a requirement. Blender CLI was preflighted after prototype approval and used successfully for three draft passes.

The story is not a feature checklist. One “Milch” objective persists from the figure's need through search, map, route and shelf. The final creative plan is nine shots / 26 seconds / 780 frames across thirteen 120 BPM bars. Its three signature moments are specified in [signature-shots.md](signature-shots.md); the timing remains testable after an authorized voice/readability prototype.
