# Three distinct directions

| Direction | Idea and strongest image | Benefit | Dependency/risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — “Der Weg wird sichtbar” | One customer's hesitation turns into a confident walk; a route travels from phone to aisle, ending with milk in hand. | Immediate human cause and effect; one actor/location; V1 search/map can inform UI. | Requires authorized footage, precise screen and body continuity. | **Selected**, with restrained mixed-reality transition from C. |
| B — “Im Flow” | Multiple high-tempo shoppers and rapid product/location match cuts synchronized to percussion. | Maximum kinetic energy, strong vertical/social cut potential. | Many actors/products/locations; product logic becomes a montage and can feel generic. | Reserve for later campaign once genuine footage library exists. |
| C — “Die Karte wird Raum” | Camera dives from phone map into a stylized spatial supermarket where route lines inhabit shelves and floor. | Distinct technical spectacle; graph/map assets translate well. | High compositing/3D burden, hands/space continuity and risk of appearing to promise AR guidance. | Borrow only one clearly cinematic match cut; do not make overlays the whole film. |

The selected approach uses one milk-search objective and one person so every shot advances the same action. It is distinct from V1's sequence of feature explanations. A controlled 32-second duration follows a 120 BPM, 16-bar music plan and the 16-shot/VO timing in the storyboard. This is a proposed edit length; a recorded read and first-viewer preview can justify a 30–35 s adjustment.
