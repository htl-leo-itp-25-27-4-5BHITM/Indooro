# Concept exploration — revised for motion graphics

The earlier selected concept required a photographed actor, real supermarket, practical phone plate and filmed reach. Its core **need → search → route → discovery** survives; its production method does not. Three fully digital directions were compared:

| Direction | Strongest image | Human clarity / premium potential | Practical risk | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — Graphic silhouette | A flat 2.5D adult silhouette moves through an elegant layered shelf field while a mint route activates. | Highly controllable, fastest Remotion-only delivery; depth may resemble an explainer if composition stays diagrammatic. | Moderate: requires artful camera parallax and clear figure poses. | **Fallback** for every B shot. |
| B — “Der Weg wird sichtbar” | A sculptural faceless shopper pauses in a modular dark store; the phone route opens the space, and the same figure reaches milk. | One human story, premium 3D depth, clear app interaction, limited assets; best balance. | Moderate: procedural figure/store and Blender/Remotion continuity need prototype proof. | **Selected.** |
| C — Full 3D spatial spectacle | Camera races through a large simulated supermarket with complex shelves, reflections and character animation. | High visual ceiling but the phone/user can be lost. | High: many models, long renders, continuity and rigging burden. | Reject for a 28 s AI-run production. |

**Production approaches:** Remotion alone can build A and all fallback shots with SVG/CSS/2.5D. Remotion + scripted Blender is the preferred execution of B: few 3D hero/environment passes, with Remotion owning story, UI, transitions and audio. A three-tool Remotion + Blender + Cinema 4D or generative-video chain offers no necessary benefit and adds fragile handoffs. Cinema 4D being installed does not make it a requirement. Blender is not currently on `PATH`; its availability is a post-approval preflight, not a blocking assumption.

The story is not a feature checklist. One “Milch” objective persists from the figure's need through search, map, route and shelf. A 28-second/840-frame proposal follows fourteen 120 BPM bars; the timing remains editable after a real voice/readability test.
