# German voice-over options — all subject to user approval

The music plan is 32 s; suggested VO windows are 0.8–2.7, 7.2–10.4, 14.2–15.9, 24.4–27.2 and 28.5–31.0. Actual duration must be measured from the chosen performance before picture lock. Target voice: conversational Austrian or neutral German, adult, close and confident, with no announcer boom or exaggerated “tech” delivery. Record at 48 kHz/24-bit mono with room tone and alternates; document performer consent/license. Readable scripts use no accuracy, savings or retailer claims.

## A — Cinematic minimal (recommended)

> Milch. Aber wo? [breath] Indooro zeigt dir das Regal. Und den Weg dorthin. [space for arrival] Ein Blick. Ein Griff. Indooro. Finde deinen Weg.

23 spoken words, about 15–18 s of natural speech distributed across the five windows; pauses and action fill the rest. This directly binds search, route and physical result, leaves room for location sound and gives the brand a clear ending. Keep `zeigt dir das Regal` only if the final screen accurately displays a shelf/product destination.

## B — Energetic and direct

> Du suchst Milch? Dann los. Indooro findet den Platz im Markt und zeigt dir den Weg. Sehen. Gehen. Finden. Indooro. Finde deinen Weg.

25 spoken words, about 14–17 s at an alert but unhurried pace. More rhythmic, suitable if the filmed walk has stronger propulsion. `findet den Platz` needs the same UI/source-truth check as A. Avoid clipped, sports-commercial shouting.

## C — Natural customer point of view

> Ich brauch noch Milch. Wo war die gleich? Okay, Indooro zeigt mir das Regal. Da lang. Gefunden. Indooro. Finde deinen Weg.

24 spoken words, about 15–19 s with conversational hesitation. This makes the person even more present but requires the pictured actor's perspective and vocal performance to feel consistent. It is less brand-neutral if the cast changes later.

**Decision:** A best preserves space for image and sound and avoids narrating every tap. Record a scratch read by an authorized speaker after approval; do not synthesize/purchase audio during planning. If using TTS later, test pronunciation of “Indooro”, German prosody and commercial rights, and secure consent for any cloned voice.
