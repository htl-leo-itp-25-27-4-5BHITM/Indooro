# Creative direction — A route becomes clarity

The visual motif is a single mint line. At first it loops through an abstract dark supermarket. It then straightens into a destination path, becomes the phone's search affordance, traces a walkable route, connects the local shopping list, outlines the admin layout, and closes as a small route glyph beside INDOORO. This makes continuity legible without copying a technology advertiser's specific design.

## Rules

- **Palette:** background `#071117`; raised map plane `#0D1B22`; panel `#11242B`; typography `#F4F7F5` and muted `#9DB0B2`; route mint `#63E6B4`; secondary teal `#27B9AB`; occasional obstacle/inefficient amber `#E8B86D`. No saturated rainbow, no red retail branding.
- **Type:** Inter or a compatible locally bundled sans. Hero wordmark 100–124 px, feature headlines 72–90 px, UI headings 32–42 px, labels minimum 22 px at 1080p. Headline copy stays under six words; left-aligned headline zones have at least 110 px frame margin.
- **Grid:** 12-column implied composition; headline at left 8–9% of width, subject right or centered according to scene. Keep 6% top/bottom safe area. One primary subject per shot.
- **Lighting:** radial blue-green pools under the phone/map; one soft key from upper-left; diffused shadows, no hard specular flare. Phone glass uses a low-opacity diagonal reflection that never covers text.
- **Depth:** near phone layer has 30–60 px soft shadow; map layer is a 2D plane with mild perspective/rotation, max 12°. UI stays front-facing for reading. Background contains quiet graph lines at under 12% opacity.
- **Motion:** standard reveal 14–20 frames with cubic ease-out, camera moves 40–90 frames, route draws 35–75 frames. Avoid elastic bounce. Stagger list rows by 5–7 frames. Hold each key interface state at least 45 frames.
- **Map:** shelves are coherent repeated rectangular blocks; walkable paths are visibly open. Beacon pulses are broad, low-opacity rings and never masquerade as precise measurement. Position circle always snaps to corridor center. Explored A* edges recede to muted teal as the chosen path is drawn in mint.
- **Interface:** simplified iOS-inspired but custom Indooro concept, dark cards, clear search field, product result, route badge, list rows. `Konzeptdarstellung` lives unobtrusively in the lower screen edge during detailed UI/admin shots.
- **Audio:** original minimal pulse in D minor at ~92 BPM, low sustained chord, light percussive ticks, soft filtered whooshes at scene boundaries, final two-note identity. Interface sounds only for product selection and list reordering.
- **Opening/closing:** start nearly black with scattered shelf strokes and a deliberately meandering amber line; end with a clean dark field, composed phone/map behind, route glyph and wordmark with the single claim `Finde deinen Weg.`

## References and limits

Reference qualities are premium technology-film restraint, crisp UI animation, and diagram clarity. There is no direct copying of a particular company's graphics. The actual repository's green/red pictorial logo informs the navigation/cart association but is not composited from its white-background PNG. The provisional film identity is documented as a video treatment. No retailer brand is shown.
