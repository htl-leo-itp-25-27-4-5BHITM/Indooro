## Why

Indooro has working and planned customer, routing, and store-management concepts but no concise film that explains the product to a first-time viewer. A 65-second product film can make the search-to-route experience understandable at a glance for technology audiences, potential partners, teachers, evaluators, and website visitors while preserving the distinction between a demonstration and verified deployment.

## What Changes

- Produce an isolated, editable Remotion project and an H.264 Full HD German-language film, preview MP4, poster, storyboard, and production report.
- Tell an eight-scene visual story: search friction, Indooro identity, product search, indoor position concept, walkable A* route, local shopping-list tour concept, administration, and closing identity.
- Use actual Indooro repository facts and a clearly illustrative interface. Do not imply a SPAR partnership, measured time savings, proven positioning accuracy, or backend-synced shopping lists.
- Introduce a reusable motion system, coherent audio where legally usable, render automation, and visual/technical review.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `film-visual-design`: Cinematic visual identity, typography, layout, and interface representation.
- `film-storytelling`: Narrative order, truthful product claims, and scene content.
- `film-animation`: Deterministic movement, camera, map, route, and transitions.
- `film-technical-production`: Editable Remotion project, reproducible rendering, and exports.
- `film-audio`: Licensed or originally produced music and synchronized effects.
- `film-quality-assurance`: Frame, transition, content, and media verification.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This production documents and illustrates Indooro; it does not change application behavior or APIs.

## Impact

New source lives in `indooro-motion/`; planning lives in this change. The original Swift, backend, admin, and deployment code remain untouched. The project uses Node, React, TypeScript, SVG, Remotion, and Remotion's FFmpeg-based export pipeline. Source facts are `README.md`, `openspec/specs/project-overview`, `product-catalog-search`, `ios-store-map-experience`, `mobile-positioning-navigation`, `ios-product-planning`, and `store-layout-management`, plus the canonical Swift app and existing logos. Success means all promised files exist, the film is 1920×1080/30 fps/65 s, major text is readable, transitions have no blank frames, and every depicted capability is framed accurately.
