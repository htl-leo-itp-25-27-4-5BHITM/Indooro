## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Audio uses authorized material
The soundtrack SHALL use self-generated tones or assets with documented permission, with no unauthorized copyrighted music. Verification: inspect the asset inventory and source recipe.

#### Scenario: Audio asset is reviewed
- **WHEN** the final mix's source files are listed
- **THEN** each source has a documented origin and usage basis

### Requirement: Audio supports the visual structure
The bed SHALL have measured scene accents, restrained interface cues, smooth beginning/end fades, and no abrupt clip. Verification: listen to opening, transitions, ending; inspect waveform peaks.

#### Scenario: Final brand reveal occurs
- **WHEN** the ending resolves
- **THEN** a subdued accent coincides with the mark reveal and the bed fades before the final frame

### Requirement: Sound is optional for comprehension
The visual film SHALL remain understandable when played without audio. Verification: muted narrative review.

#### Scenario: Preview is muted
- **WHEN** all eight scenes play without sound
- **THEN** text and choreography still communicate the search-to-route story
