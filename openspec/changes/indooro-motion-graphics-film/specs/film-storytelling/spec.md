## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The narrative follows a clear product arc
The film SHALL show the search problem, Indooro identity, product search, position and single route, local multi-stop concept, supporting administration, and final identity in that order. Verification: compare rendered key frames with `storyboard.md`.

#### Scenario: Viewer watches without sound
- **WHEN** the film is played muted
- **THEN** its major on-screen claims and map interactions still explain why Indooro exists and what it aims to do

### Requirement: Claims reflect repository evidence
The film SHALL avoid verified accuracy, measured time savings, official retailer partnership, production deployment, and universal shortest multi-stop-route claims. It SHALL treat the mobile list/tour as local assistance and administration as a supporting system. Verification: transcribe all visible claims and compare them to the cited current OpenSpec capabilities.

#### Scenario: Shopping-list example is presented
- **WHEN** the four sample items are reordered on the map
- **THEN** the film demonstrates only that this illustrated route becomes shorter and does not state a measured real-world outcome

### Requirement: Opening and ending resolve visually
The film SHALL establish search friction in the first six seconds and hold the final Indooro mark and claim long enough to read. Verification: inspect the opening and the last 90 frames.

#### Scenario: Final shot ends
- **WHEN** frame 1949 is reached
- **THEN** the mark and claim are fully visible without a mid-animation cutoff
